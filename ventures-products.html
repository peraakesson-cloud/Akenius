02How a venture is built

How a venture is built

  1. 01

    Validate

    Market and competitor mapping, customer discovery, supplier quotes at real volumes. Spend stays close to zero.

  2. 02

    Gate

    A go/no-go decision against fixed criteria: verified unit economics, genuine audience pull, committed capital, clear IP.

  3. 03

    Make it sellable

    Company, partner agreement, IP, regulatory and product documentation, so the product can be sold legally across the EU.

  4. 04

    Launch and scale

    A measured launch, acquisition cost proven before spend increases, then new markets and channels.

03How ventures are structured

How ventures are structured

Its own company.

Each venture runs in a separate company, so risk stays where it belongs.

IP held by Akenius.

Brands and know-how sit in Akenius and are licensed to the venture.

Owned with a partner.

Every venture has a co-owner, with roles, capital and exit agreed in writing from day one.
