A Swedish holding company building product businesses for Europe — patiently, and with our own capital at stake.

Most good products don’t fail on the shelf. They fail on pricing, on sourcing, on channels — and on the patience to get them right. Akenius brings operating experience to that gap, and stays long enough for it to matter.

01How Akenius works

i

Few, and for the long run.

We commit to a handful of ventures and stay for years, not quarters.

ii

Proof before capital.

Every venture passes a validation gate — verified costs and real customer pull — before serious money is committed.

iii

Ownership, not fees.

We earn through equity and results, alongside the partners we build with.

04About

One owner. A long horizon.

Akenius AB was founded in 2026 by Per Åkesson. Over 25 years at VELUX he led the Nordic business with full P&L responsibility, ran global pricing, and took a digital B2C customer journey from concept to operation across several markets. He serves on the board of Kemp & Lauritzen A/S and holds an EMBA from Lund University, with executive education at INSEAD.

The name comes from his grandfather Åke — an entrepreneur, as was his father before him. Akenius is the third generation.
